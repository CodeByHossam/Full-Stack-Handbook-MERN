Software Development Lifecycle (SDLC)

Phases before and around implementation, and how Agile/Waterfall/Kanban compare.

Complete phases (pre-implementation focus)

  1. Business idea & problem discovery — Validate real problem, not just an idea.
  2. Business feasibility & validation — Technical, operational, financial, legal, schedule.
  3. Requirements elicitation & analysis — Stakeholders, needs, constraints.
  4. Scope definition & prioritization — In/out, MVP, trade-offs.
  5. System analysis & domain modeling — Flows, entities, invariants.
  6. Software architecture & technical design — Layers, boundaries, ADRs.
  7. Database & API design — Contracts, models, authZ/N.
  8. UI/UX research & design — Journeys, wireframes, states.
  9. Project structure & engineering standards — Conventions, repo layout.
  10. Project planning, estimation & scheduling — WBS, milestones, capacity.
  11. Risk analysis & quality planning — Risks, gates, testing strategy.
  12. Development readiness review — Go/no-go gate before coding.
  13. Implementation — Build to contracts.
  14. Testing — Unit/integration/E2E, acceptance.
  15. Deployment — Release with guardrails.
  16. Maintenance — Monitoring, fixes, evolution.

Methodologies

ApproachWhen suitableNotes
WaterfallFixed scope, regulated, low uncertaintySequential; harder to change late.
Agile (Scrum)Evolving scope, feedback-drivenIterative, time-boxed; emphasize DoR/DoD.
KanbanContinuous flow, support/ops-heavyVisualize WIP, optimize flow.
Hybrid/PragmaticMost real projectsAdapt: plan enough upfront, iterate continuously.
Note

Match process to size, risk, regulatory needs, and uncertainty — not dogma.
